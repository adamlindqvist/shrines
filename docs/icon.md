# Shrines icon

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The turquoise puzzle block and golden sun echo the game's main interaction. The opaque square artwork is resized for the favicon, Apple touch icon, and 192/512 px installation icons. The separate maskable icon adds padding to keep the symbol inside adaptive masks.

Source: `shrines-icon-source.png`. Runtime files: `../public/icons/`, `../public/apple-touch-icon.png`, and `../public/favicon.png`.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: square home-screen game icon for Shrines, a warm playful low-poly fantasy puzzle adventure. Primary request: a striking extremely simple recognizable icon: one chunky turquoise stone puzzle cube in a gentle three-quarter view, with a bold sunshine-gold sun emblem inset on its front face. Soft bevels, tactile polished clay-like faceted rendering, crisp large silhouette, restrained warm lighting. Background is opaque solid deep clover green, full bleed to every edge. Center the entire cube and emblem inside the central 65% of the square so circular and rounded-square app masks cannot clip it. Limited palette turquoise, sunshine gold, deep clover green. No text, letters, border, pre-rounded outer corners, scenery, particles, additional objects, watermark, or fine details. Readable at 32 pixels. Generate one square 1024 by 1024 image.
